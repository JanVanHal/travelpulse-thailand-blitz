## Plugs and voltage

- **Page:** `living.html` (new “Power” section; the guide did not state voltage or plug type)
- **Old:** not on the page
- **New:** Mains supply is 220 volts at 50 hertz. MEA states 220 volts as the standard line voltage, and household single-phase supply as 220–230 volts. Thai grounded plug is Type O (three round pins). Sockets also accept Type A, B and C in practice. UK Type G and Australian Type I need an adapter. A charger marked 100–240V does not need a converter.
- **Source:** https://www.mea.or.th/electricity/electricity-tariffs/measafety-3pinplug-18april2025 (MEA: Thailand standard 220 volts, 18 April 2025); https://www.mea.or.th/electricity/energy-saving-tips/KyoG1huER (single-phase 220–230 volts); https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Updated-Regulatory-Regime-of-Thailand-July-2022_clean-ver.pdf (Thailand mains frequency 50 Hz); https://www.mea.or.th/electricity/electricity-tariffs/mea-aroundtheworld (Type O used in Thailand); https://www.worldstandards.eu/electricity/plug-voltage-by-country/thailand/ (Types A, B, C and O in use). Voltage in the guide follows MEA, not the 230 V figure on that plug chart.
